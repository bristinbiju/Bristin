Three accent options, taken from your own work

Each option is a single accent used only for buttons, links and key words, on the new blue-charcoal background (#14191C) with light #EDEDED sections. Headings are set in Syne and body text in Be Vietnam Pro. Every pairing shown passes WCAG AA.

Fresh, calm, healthcare and growth

Taken from the World Cancer Day ribbon and the Liverpool clinic teal. It is the closest to your reference mood and pairs with an aqua support tone (#73E0E3). It feels fresh and trustworthy, but it is the most "tech-startup" of the three.
